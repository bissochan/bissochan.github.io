## Problem

Part of a Software Engineering challenge run with the **City of Trento**: help citizens understand and improve how waste is collected and separated in the city.

## What we built

- **Real-time tracking** of waste collection trucks, so residents can see where collection is happening.
- **Urban decay reports**: residents report overflowing bins or improper disposal so the city can act on them.
- **Separation insights**: a view of the areas where waste is separated and recycled correctly.
- **Community engagement**: users can follow reports and see how things improve over time.
- **Bulky-waste bookings**: appointments to dispose of items such as used oil, sofas or TVs.

## What I learned

My first full-stack project in a team from requirements to deployment: working with a real public stakeholder, splitting the work, and packaging the app with Docker.
